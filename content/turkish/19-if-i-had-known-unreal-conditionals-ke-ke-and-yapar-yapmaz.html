If I Had Known... — Unreal Conditionals, Keşke, and Yapar Yapmaz

This final lesson completes the Turkish conditional system with the "unreal" and "past" conditional (used for hypothetical or regretful situations), the word keşke for expressing wishes and regrets, the handy pattern for saying "as soon as," and a last set of vocabulary-building endings to round out your ability to recognize new words on your own.

Grammar

  • The second and third conditional (-seydi): in Lesson 13 you learned the present conditional (-se combined with the present "to be"). Now combine -se- with the PAST tense of "to be" instead to get a second set of "if" endings, used for hypothetical or counterfactual situations.
    gelseydim - if I had come / if I came (hypothetically)
    yapsaydın - if you had done / if you did
    görseydiniz - if you had seen / if you saw This single form covers two related English meanings, distinguished by context:
    "Unreal" conditional (about the future, unlikely to happen): Yerinde olsaydım giderdim. - If I were you, I would go.
    Past conditional (about something that might have happened, but
    didn't): Yerinde olsaydım giderdim. - If I had been you, I would have gone. Note that in BOTH readings, the result clause uses the habitual past (-erdi, from Lesson 17) — but here it should be translated as "would do" or "would have done," NOT as "used to do." Compare gelirsen memnun olurum (if you come, I'll be happy — a simple, real possibility) with gelseydin memnun olurdum (if you came/had come, I would be/have been happy — expresses doubt about whether it will or did happen).
  • Keşke ("if only... / I wish..."): pairs with the past conditional form to express regret or a wish.
    Keşke gitmeseydin. - I wish you hadn't gone.
    Keşke daha güzel olsaydım! - If only I were prettier!
    Keşke hiç tanışmasaydık! - I wish we had never met!
  • Yapar yapmaz ("as soon as"): place the positive and negative -r present forms of the same verb back-to-back.
    Eve gelir gelmez banyo yapacağım. - I'll have a bath the minute I get
    home.
    Telefon çalar çalmaz cevap ver! - Answer straight away when the
    phone rings!
  • More vocabulary-building endings: a final set, rounding out the pattern from Lesson 18, to help you recognize and eventually build new words from verb stems on your own.
    -mez: değişmek (to change) -> değişmez (unchanging, stable)
    -ç: kazanmak (to win) -> kazanç (profit, gain)
    -gıç: başlamak (to start) -> başlangıç (the start)
    -ik: bozmak (to break) -> bozuk (broken)
    -ek: uçmak (to fly) -> uçak (airplane)
    -it: geçmek (to pass) -> geçit (mountain pass, passage)
    -ıntı: akmak (to flow) -> akıntı (current)

Examples

Fiyatları bilseydim orada yemezdik.

fee-yaht-lah-RUH beel-sey-DEEM oh-rah-DAH yeh-mez-DEEK

If I had known the prices, we wouldn't have eaten there.

Vapurla gitseydik iki kat uzun sürerdi.

vah-poor-LAH geet-sey-DEEK ee-KEE kaht oo-ZOON sew-rer-DEE

If we'd gone by ferry it would have taken twice as long.

Keşke gitmeseydin.

kesh-KEH geet-meh-sey-DEEN

I wish you hadn't gone.

Eve gelir gelmez telefon edeceğim.

eh-VEH geh-LEER gel-MEZ teh-leh-FOHN eh-deh-jeh-EEM

I'll call as soon as I get home.

Hesabı görseydi, beni öldürürdü!

heh-sah-BUH gur-sey-DEE, beh-NEE ur-lew-rewr-DEW

If she had seen the bill, she would have killed me!

Notes

Congratulations on reaching the final unit — the grammar covered across these 19 lessons (from var/yok all the way to unreal conditionals) takes you to a genuine intermediate level of Turkish, roughly the Council of Europe's Waystage/B1 benchmark the source textbook targets. From here, the best next steps are heavy exposure: reading, listening to Turkish media, and above all speaking — the -miş, -dik, and conditional patterns in particular only become truly natural through repeated real use.

Practice

  1. Build the -seydi form of bilmek for "if I had known."
  2. Translate using keşke: "I wish we had never met."
  3. Say "I'll call you as soon as I arrive" using yapar yapmaz-style repetition of gelmek.
  4. Explain the difference between gelirsen memnun olurum and gelseydin memnun olurdum.
  5. Build a noun from uçmak (to fly) meaning "airplane" using -ek.
