Adjectives — Agreement, Placement, and Colors

French adjectives must match the noun they describe in gender and number, and most (but not all) go after the noun — this lesson covers the rules and the main exceptions.

Grammar

  • Basic agreement: add -e for feminine (if the masculine doesn't already end in -e), add -s for plural (silent in speech): grand -> grande -> grands -> grandes.
  • Irregular feminine patterns: -eux -> -euse (heureux -> heureuse); -f -> -ve (actif -> active); -on/-en -> double consonant + e (bon -> bonne, ancien -> ancienne); -c -> -che or -que (blanc -> blanche); a handful are fully irregular (beau -> belle, nouveau -> nouvelle, vieux -> vieille, long -> longue).
  • Adjectives with a special form before a masculine noun starting with a vowel/mute h: beau -> bel (un bel homme), nouveau -> nouvel (un nouvel ami), vieux -> vieil (un vieil arbre) — this extra form exists purely for pronunciation.
  • Placement: most adjectives follow the noun (une voiture rouge). The BAGS adjectives (Beauty, Age, Goodness, Size — beau, joli, jeune, vieux, nouveau, bon, mauvais, grand, petit, gros, and a few more like haut, long, meilleur) come BEFORE the noun.
  • A few adjectives change MEANING depending on placement: un homme grand (a tall man) vs. un grand homme (a great man); ma propre chambre (my own room) vs. une chambre propre (a clean room); un ancien professeur (a former teacher) vs. un livre ancien (an old/ancient book).
  • Colors: agree like regular adjectives (une robe bleue) EXCEPT compound colors and colors derived from nouns (fruits, objects), which stay INVARIABLE: des yeux bleu clair (light blue eyes — invariable), des robes orange (orange dresses — invariable, from the noun "orange").

Examples

C'est une belle maison ancienne.

It's a beautiful old house. (belle = BAGS, before; ancienne here means "old/historic," after the noun)

Il porte une chemise bleu marine.

He's wearing a navy blue shirt. (compound color, invariable)

Mon ancien collègue habite maintenant à Nice.

My former colleague now lives in Nice. (meaning-shift placement: "ancien" before = "former")

Elle a les cheveux longs et les yeux verts.

She has long hair and green eyes.

Notes

  • When two adjectives (one BAGS, one not) both describe the same noun, each stays in its normal position: une petite voiture rouge (a little red car).

Practice

Make these agree correctly: un(e) (grand) maison, des (beau) fleurs, une femme (heureux), des chaussures (marron - note: invariable, from the noun "chestnut").
