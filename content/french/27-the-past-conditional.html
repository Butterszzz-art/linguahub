The Past Conditional

The past conditional expresses "would have" — used for hypothetical situations in the past that didn't actually happen, and for regrets.

Grammar

  • Formation: present conditional of avoir or être (same helper-verb choice as usual) + past participle.
  • Used for:
    1. Hypothetical past results, paired with "si + plus-que-parfait": Si j'avais su, je serais venu. (If I had known, I would have come.)
    2. Regret or reproach: J'aurais dû t'appeler. (I should have called you. — see Lesson 28 for devoir nuances)
    3. Unconfirmed past information (reported speech): L'accident aurait été causé par la pluie. (The accident was reportedly caused by the rain.)
  • Agreement rules for the past participle follow the passé composé pattern.

Reference Table

parler (to speak) — past conditional:
j'aurais parlé          nous aurions parlé
tu aurais parlé           vous auriez parlé
il/elle/on aurait parlé    ils/elles auraient parlé

Vocabulary

si + plus-que-parfait, ...conditionnel passé
if (had)..., would have...
j'aurais dû
I should have
j'aurais aimé / voulu
I would have liked

Examples

Si j'avais eu le temps, je t'aurais aidé.

If I had had time, I would have helped you.

Elle aurait aimé venir, mais elle était malade.

She would have liked to come, but she was sick.

Vous n'auriez pas dû partir si tôt.

You shouldn't have left so early.

Nous serions arrivés à l'heure sans les embouteillages.

We would have arrived on time if not for the traffic.

Notes

  • The three-tense "if-chain" is a common exam/conversation stumbling block — memorize it as a set: si + présent -> présent/futur/impératif; si + imparfait -> conditionnel présent; si + plus-que-parfait -> conditionnel passé.
