Planning a Trip and Money Matters

Vocabulary for booking travel, budgeting, and handling money — banks, currency exchange, and everyday financial phrases.

Grammar

  • "Il faut que + subjunctive" (Lesson 29) is common for trip-planning necessities: Il faut que je réserve mon billet. (I need to book my ticket.)
  • Currency amounts follow the noun: cinquante euros (fifty euros) — Lesson 5's numbers apply directly here.

Examples

J'ai réservé mon billet d'avion pour le quinze juin.

I booked my plane ticket for June 15th.

Où est le distributeur le plus proche ?

Where is the nearest ATM?

Je voudrais changer des dollars en euros.

I would like to exchange dollars for euros.

N'oublie pas de vérifier la date d'expiration de ton passeport.

Don't forget to check your passport's expiration date.

Notes

  • Many ATMs and exchange counters in France use the term "distributeur" or "DAB" (distributeur automatique de billets) rather than a direct translation of "ATM."
