The Futur Antérieur

The futur antérieur ("future perfect") expresses an action that WILL HAVE happened before another future action or a specific future point in time.

Grammar

  • Formation: futur simple of avoir or être (same helper-verb choice as passé composé) + past participle.
  • Used to show that one future event will be completed before another future event: Quand tu arriveras, j'aurai déjà préparé le dîner. (When you arrive, I will have already prepared dinner.)
  • Very commonly follows "quand/lorsque/dès que/après que" when the action needs to be presented as completed before the other future action: Dès que j'aurai fini, je te le dirai. (As soon as I've finished, I'll tell you.)
  • Agreement rules for the past participle follow the same pattern as passé composé (Lesson 18–19).

Reference Table

parler (to speak) — futur antérieur:
j'aurai parlé          nous aurons parlé
tu auras parlé           vous aurez parlé
il/elle/on aura parlé    ils/elles auront parlé

Vocabulary

dès que / aussitôt que
as soon as
après que
after
avant + [future time]
before

Examples

Nous aurons fini le projet avant vendredi.

We will have finished the project before Friday.

Quand elle aura vingt-cinq ans, elle aura déjà voyagé partout.

By the time she's 25, she'll already have traveled everywhere.

Dès que j'aurai reçu ta lettre, je répondrai.

As soon as I've received your letter, I'll reply.

Notes

  • The futur antérieur and futur simple often appear paired in the same sentence, one showing what happens first (futur antérieur), the other showing what happens as a result (futur simple).
