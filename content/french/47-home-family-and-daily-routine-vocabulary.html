Home, Family, and Daily Routine Vocabulary

Core vocabulary for describing where you live, who's in your family, and your daily schedule — pairs naturally with the reflexive verbs from Lesson 14 and possessive adjectives from Lesson 42.

Grammar

  • "Habiter" (to live/reside) takes no preposition before a city, but uses à/dans/en as covered in Lesson 36 for locations: J'habite à Paris dans un petit appartement.
  • Rooms and house vocabulary combine naturally with "dans le/la" (in the): dans la cuisine, dans le salon.
  • Possessive adjectives (Lesson 42) are essential for family vocabulary, since you're almost always talking about MY mother, HIS brother, etc.

Examples

Dans mon appartement, il y a deux chambres et une petite cuisine.

In my apartment, there are two bedrooms and a small kitchen.

Mon père travaille et ma mère reste à la maison.

My father works and my mother stays home.

Nous dînons ensemble en famille tous les soirs.

We have dinner together as a family every evening.

J'ai deux frères et une sœur.

I have two brothers and a sister.

Notes

  • Combine this vocabulary with Lesson 14's reflexive verbs to describe a full daily routine: Je me réveille, je me lave dans la salle de bains, je prends le petit-déjeuner dans la cuisine...
