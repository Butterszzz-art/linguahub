Getting Around and Finding a Place to Stay

Transportation vocabulary (planes, trains, taxis) and lodging vocabulary (hotels, reservations, check-in/out).

Grammar

  • "Prendre" is the standard verb for taking a mode of transport: prendre le train, prendre un taxi, prendre l'avion.
  • Reserving a room: "Je voudrais réserver une chambre" (conditional for politeness, Lesson 26) + details (pour deux personnes, avec vue sur mer, etc.).

Examples

Un aller-retour pour Lyon, s'il vous plaît.

A round-trip ticket to Lyon, please.

De quel quai part le train pour Marseille ?

Which platform does the train for Marseille leave from?

Je voudrais réserver une chambre double pour trois nuits.

I would like to book a double room for three nights.

À quelle heure est le check-out ?

What time is check-out?

Notes

  • French train stations use "quai" (platform) and "voie" (track) — both appear on departure boards, so it's worth recognizing both terms.
