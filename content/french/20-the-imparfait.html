The Imparfait

The imparfait ("imperfect") is French's other main past tense — used for descriptions, background, habits, and ongoing states in the past, as opposed to the completed actions of the passé composé.

Grammar

  • Formation: take the nous form of the present tense, drop -ons, and add:
    -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient (This works for every verb except être, whose stem is irregular: ét-.)
  • Uses of the imparfait:
    1. Descriptions in the past (people, places, weather, states of mind): Il faisait beau. (The weather was nice.)
    2. Habitual/repeated past actions ("used to," "would [routinely]"): Quand j'étais petit, je jouais dehors tous les jours. (When I was little, I used to play outside every day.)
    3. An ongoing action interrupted by another action (often paired with passé composé for the interrupting event): Je dormais quand le téléphone a sonné. (I was sleeping when the phone rang.)
    4. Polite requests/suggestions: Je voulais vous demander... (I wanted to ask you...)

Reference Table

parler (to speak) — imparfait:
je parlais          nous parlions
tu parlais           vous parliez
il/elle/on parlait    ils/elles parlaient

être (to be) — imparfait (irregular stem ét-):
j'étais    tu étais    il était    nous étions    vous étiez    ils étaient

Vocabulary

souvent

Examples

Quand j'étais enfant, j'habitais à la campagne.

When I was a child, I lived in the countryside.

Il pleuvait quand nous sommes sortis.

It was raining when we went out.

Nous allions à la plage tous les étés.

We used to go to the beach every summer.

Elle était fatiguée après le travail.

She was tired after work.

Notes

  • Weather, age, time, and emotional/physical states are almost always in the imparfait when narrating the past, because they describe a background condition rather than a completed event.
