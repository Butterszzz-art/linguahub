Going Out on the Town

Vocabulary for making plans, extending and accepting/declining invitations, and enjoying evening entertainment.

Grammar

  • Extending an invitation: "Ça te dit de...?" (informal, "Does it appeal to you to...?") or "Voudrais-tu / Voudriez-vous...?" (Would you like to...?) + infinitive.
  • Accepting: "Avec plaisir !" (With pleasure!) / "Bonne idée !" (Good idea!) Declining politely: "Je suis désolé(e), mais je ne peux pas..." (I'm sorry, but I can't...) + infinitive.
  • "On se retrouve..." (Let's meet up..., lit. "we find each other again") is a natural way to arrange a meeting point/time.

Examples

Ça te dit d'aller au cinéma ce soir ?

Do you feel like going to the movies tonight?

Avec plaisir, à quelle heure ?

With pleasure, what time?

On se retrouve devant le théâtre à vingt heures.

Let's meet in front of the theater at 8 PM.

Je suis désolé, je ne peux pas venir, j'ai déjà des projets.

I'm sorry, I can't come, I already have plans.
