The Genitive Case

The genitive expresses possession — one noun belonging to another, like the English "-'s." It's the least common of the four cases in everyday spoken German, often replaced informally by a simpler construction, but you'll see and hear it regularly enough (especially in writing) that it's worth knowing properly.

Grammar

Use the genitive to show that one noun belongs to or is associated with another:

  • Das ist die Tasche meines Vaters. (That is my father's bag.)

Word order is the opposite of English: in English, the possessor comes first ("my father's bag"); in German, the possessor typically follows the noun it modifies (die Tasche meines Vaters — literally "the bag of my father").

Genitive endings:

ModifierMasculineFeminineNeuterPlural
definitedes Mannesder Fraudes Kindesder Gäste
indefiniteeines Manneseiner Fraueines Kindes—
possessivemeines Mannesmeiner Fraumeines Kindesmeiner Gäste

Masculine and neuter nouns add -es (for short, one-syllable nouns like Mann → Mannes) or just -s (for longer nouns like Computer → Computers) in addition to changing the article. Feminine and plural nouns take no ending at all on the noun itself — only the article changes.

Unlike English, German never uses an apostrophe before this -s, even with proper names:

  • Das ist Peters Buch. (This is Peter's book.) — not "Peter's Buch"

A small set of prepositions take the genitive: trotz (despite), während (during), wegen (because of), (an)statt (instead of).

  • Während seiner Zeit ... (During his time ...)
  • Trotz ihres hohen Alters ... (Despite her old age ...)

An important practical note: in everyday spoken German, the genitive is often felt to be formal or old-fashioned, and is commonly replaced with von + dative instead:

  • Formal/written: Das ist das Auto meines Bruders.
  • Common in speech: Das ist das Auto von meinem Bruder.

Both are correct; the genitive is more common in writing, von + dative is more common in casual conversation.

Examples

Das ist das Auto meiner Tante.

That is my aunt's car.

Das ist die Meinung der Gäste.

That is the guests' opinion.

Das ist Peters Buch.

This is Peter's book. (no apostrophe)

Wegen des Wetters bleiben wir zu Hause.

Because of the weather, we're staying home.

Das Auto meines Bruders → Das Auto von meinem Bruder

the same idea, formal genitive vs. everyday spoken alternative.

Notes

At a beginner-to-intermediate level, you'll get far more mileage from recognizing the genitive when you read or hear it than from producing it fluently yourself — leaning on von + dative in your own speech is completely natural and won't mark you as incorrect.
