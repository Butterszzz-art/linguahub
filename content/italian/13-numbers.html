Numbers

Numbers show up everywhere — prices, phone numbers, ages, addresses — so this lesson builds the full counting system from zero through the millions, plus the two irregular quirks worth knowing up front.

Grammar

  • Numbers 0-10: zero, uno, due, tre, quattro, cinque, sei, sette, otto, nove, dieci
  • Numbers 11-19 are irregular and must be memorized individually: undici, dodici, tredici, quattordici, quindici, sedici, diciassette, diciotto, diciannove
  • From 20 on, numbers are built by combining a "tens" word with a "ones" word, exactly like English (twenty-two): venti + due = ventidue.
  • When a "tens" word ends in a vowel and meets uno or otto, the tens word DROPS its final vowel: venti + uno = ventuno (not ventiuno); quaranta + otto = quarantotto (not quarantaotto).
  • Tens: venti (20), trenta (30), quaranta (40), cinquanta (50), sessanta (60), settanta (70), ottanta (80), novanta (90), cento (100)
  • Hundreds simply prefix cento: duecento (200), trecento (300), and so on — no space, no "e."
  • Thousands: mille (1,000) is irregular in the plural — it becomes -mila: duemila (2,000), tremila (3,000). Millions and billions: un milione (1,000,000, note the different word), un miliardo (1,000,000,000).
  • Decades as a period of time ("the sixties") need a phrase, not a single word: gli anni sessanta (literally "the years sixty").

Examples

Ho trentatré anni.

oh trehn-tah-TREH AHN-nee

I'm thirty-three years old.

Il biglietto costa quarantotto euro.

eel bee-LYEHT-toh KOHS-tah kwah-rahn-TOHT-toh EH-oo-roh

The ticket costs forty-eight euros.

Ci sono duemilaventisei studenti.

chee SOH-noh doo-eh-mee-lah-vehn-tee- SEY stoo-DEHN-tee

There are two thousand and twenty-six students.

Il mio numero è zero-tre-tre...

eel MEE-oh NOO-meh-roh eh DZEH-roh treh treh

My number is oh-three-three...

Notes

  • The uno/otto vowel-drop rule (ventuno, trentotto, quarantuno, cinquantotto) trips up learners constantly — practice it deliberately rather than defaulting to the "expected" full form.
  • Unlike English, Italian doesn't insert "and" between hundreds and the rest of the number: 322 is trecentoventidue, with no word for "and."

Practice

Say these numbers aloud: 21, 38, 47, 96, 100, 250, 1,984, 3,000,000.
