Going Out — Movies, Theater, Museums, and Festivals

Italians enjoy an active cultural and social life — outdoor festivals, local sagre celebrating regional food, film, and theater. This lesson gives you the vocabulary and questions to plan an evening out.

Grammar

  • To ask what's happening or what's playing: Cosa c'è da fare di sera? (What is there to do in the evenings?) Cosa danno? (What's playing? — literally "what are they showing," used for movies)
  • To ask about tickets and timing: Dove si comprano i biglietti? (Where do you buy tickets?) A che ora comincia lo spettacolo? (What time does the show start?) Ci sono ancora posti? (Are there still seats available?)
  • Suggesting an activity together uses the noi form of andare as a question, without needing a separate "let's" word: Andiamo al cinema? (Shall we go to the movies?) — the noi form alone carries the "let's" meaning.
  • Common venues: il cinema (movie theater), il teatro (theater), il museo (museum), la sagra (a local food/harvest festival), il concerto (concert).

Examples

Andiamo al cinema stasera?

ahn-DYAH-moh ahl CHEE-neh-mah stah-SEH-rah

Shall we go to the movies tonight?

A che ora comincia lo spettacolo? Alle nove.

ah keh OH-rah koh-MEEN- chah loh speht-TAH-koh-loh? AHL-leh NOH-veh

What time does the show start? At nine.

Ci sono ancora biglietti per il concerto?

chee SOH-noh AHN-koh-rah bee-LYEHT-tee pehr eel kohn-CHEHR-toh

Are there still tickets for the concert?

Il film è così divertente!

eel feelm eh koh-ZEE dee-vehr-TEHN-teh

The movie is so much fun!

Notes

  • "Andiamo...?" as a question is a natural, common way to propose doing something together — you don't need a separate word for "let's," the noi verb form does that work.
  • American films shown in Italy are usually dubbed (doppiati) rather than subtitled; asking Cosa danno in lingua originale? (What's playing in the original language?) helps if you want to watch something undubbed.

Practice

Plan an evening out: propose an activity with "Andiamo...?", ask what time it starts, and ask if tickets are still available.
