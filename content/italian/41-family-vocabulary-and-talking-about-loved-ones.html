Family Vocabulary and Talking About Loved Ones

This lesson expands the family vocabulary introduced with madre/padre in Lesson 37 into a full family tree, and practices possessives (Lesson 37) and adjectives (Lesson 6) together to describe relatives in detail.

Grammar

  • Full family vocabulary follows regular gender patterns (Lesson 4): most male relatives end in -o, most female relatives end in -a, with a handful of invariable or irregular forms (il/la nipote covers both "nephew/niece" and "grandson/granddaughter," distinguished only by context or article).
  • Review: singular, unmodified family terms drop the article with possessives (Lesson 37): mio fratello, tua sorella, sua zia — but the article returns for plurals or when the noun is modified by an adjective: i miei fratelli, la mia sorella minore (my younger sister).
  • To describe family relationships and resemblances, common constructions include: assomigliare a (to resemble): Assomiglio a mio padre. (I resemble my father.) and avere ... anni (Lesson 9) for ages: Mio fratello ha ventidue anni. (My brother is twenty-two.)
  • Marital status vocabulary: sposato/a (married), single (invariable, borrowed from English), divorziato/a (divorced), fidanzato/a (engaged/ boyfriend-girlfriend).

Examples

Ho due fratelli e una sorella minore.

oh DOO-eh frah-TEHL-lee eh OO- nah soh-REHL-lah mee-NOH-reh

I have two brothers and a younger sister.

I miei nonni abitano ancora in Toscana.

ee MYEH-ee NOHN-nee AH-bee- tah-noh ahn-KOH-rah een tohs-KAH-nah

My grandparents still live in Tuscany.

Assomiglio molto a mia madre.

ahs-soh-MEE-lyoh MOHL-toh ah MEE-ah MAH-dreh

I look a lot like my mother.

Mio cugino si è sposato l'anno scorso.

MEE-oh koo-JEE-noh see eh spoh-ZAH-toh LAHN-noh SKOHR-soh

My cousin got married last year.

Notes

  • Il/la nipote covers three different English relationships (nephew, niece, grandchild) — context or an added clarifying phrase (nipote di mio fratello, "my brother's nephew/niece") resolves the ambiguity when it matters.
  • Combine this lesson with Lesson 6's adjectives freely — describing family members (mia sorella maggiore è molto simpatica) is some of the most natural practice ground for agreement rules.

Practice

Describe your immediate family (at least four relatives) including one age, one resemblance, and one adjective describing personality for each person.
