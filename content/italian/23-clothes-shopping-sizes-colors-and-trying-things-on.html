Clothes Shopping — Sizes, Colors, and Trying Things On

Italy is famous for fashion, and shopping for clothes involves its own vocabulary for sizes, colors, fabrics, and the phrases used in a fitting room. This lesson also reviews how color adjectives agree (or don't) with the noun they describe.

Grammar

  • Colors behave in three different ways for agreement, which is worth knowing at a glance:
    Fully agreeing (four endings, like Lesson 6's Type 1 adjectives): rosso/a/i/e, giallo/a/i/e, bianco/a/i/e, nero/a/i/e, grigio/a/i/e, azzurro/a/i/e
    Agreeing only in number (Type 2, -e/-i): verde/verdi
    Invariable (never change at all, regardless of gender or number): blu, rosa, marrone, viola, beige, arancione
  • Clothing sizes (la taglia) and shoe sizes (il numero) are different words in Italian — don't mix them up: Che taglia porta? (What size do you wear? — clothes) vs. Che numero porta? (What size do you wear? — shoes).
  • To ask to try something on: Posso provare...? (May I try on...?) In a dressing room, i camerini.
  • Useful fit vocabulary: stretto/a (tight), largo/a (loose), lungo/a (long), corto/a (short) — all Type 1, four-ending adjectives.

Examples

Posso provare questa gonna blu?

POHS-soh proh-VAH-reh KWEHS-tah GOHN-nah bloo

May I try on this blue skirt?

Che taglia porta? La quarantadue.

keh TAH-lyah POHR-tah? lah kwah-rahn-tah-DOO-eh

What size do you wear? A 42.

È troppo stretta; ha una taglia più grande?

eh TROHP-poh STREHT-tah; ah OO-nah TAH-lyah pyoo GRAHN-deh

It's too tight; do you have a bigger size?

Cerco una giacca sportiva blu e pantaloni bianchi.

CHEHR-koh OO-nah JAHK-kah spohr-TEE-vah bloo eh pahn-tah-LOH-nee BYAHN-kee

I'm looking for a casual blue jacket and white pants.

Notes

  • Italian clothing sizes don't map 1:1 to US/UK sizes (an Italian 42 is roughly a US 6 in women's dresses) — when shopping for real, check a conversion chart rather than assuming your home size.
  • Remember the three-way agreement split for colors above: getting rosso right (agrees fully) and blu right (never changes) both matter for sounding natural.

Practice

Describe an outfit using at least four clothing items and four different colors, making sure each color agrees correctly (or doesn't, if invariable).
