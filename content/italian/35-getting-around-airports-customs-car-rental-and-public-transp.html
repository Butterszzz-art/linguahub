Getting Around — Airports, Customs, Car Rental, and Public Transportation

This lesson bundles the vocabulary you need to physically get from place to place in Italy: through the airport, past customs, renting a car if needed, and using trains, buses, and taxis.

Grammar

  • Airport vocabulary and check-in phrases: fare il check-in (to check in), il volo (flight), l'imbarco (boarding), il bagaglio (luggage), la carta d'imbarco (boarding pass).
  • Customs: la dogana (customs). Common question: Ha qualcosa da dichiarare? (Do you have anything to declare?)
  • Renting a car uses noleggiare (to rent, for vehicles specifically — distinct from affittare, used for renting apartments/rooms): Vorrei noleggiare una macchina. (I'd like to rent a car.)
  • Public transportation: il treno (train), l'autobus (bus), il tram (tram), il taxi (taxi), la fermata (the stop). Before boarding many Italian buses and trams, you must validate (obliterare/convalidare) your ticket in a small machine on board — an unvalidated ticket, even a purchased one, can result in a fine.
  • To ask about schedules and platforms: A che ora parte il treno per Firenze? (What time does the train for Florence leave?) Da che binario? (From which platform?)

Examples

A che ora parte il volo per Roma?

ah keh OH-rah PAHR-teh eel VOH-loh pehr ROH-mah

What time does the flight for Rome leave?

Ha qualcosa da dichiarare? No, niente.

ah kwahl-KOH-zah dah dee-kyah-RAH-reh? noh, NYEHN-teh

Do you have anything to declare? No, nothing.

Vorrei noleggiare una macchina per una settimana.

vohr-RAY noh-lehj-JAH-reh OO-nah MAHK-kee-nah pehr OO-nah seht-tee-MAH-nah

I'd like to rent a car for a week.

Non dimenticare di convalidare il biglietto!

nohn dee-mehn-tee-KAH-reh dee kohn-vah-lee-DAH-reh eel bee-LYEHT-toh

Don't forget to validate the ticket!

Notes

  • Ticket validation is a real and commonly-missed step for visitors — buying a ticket alone is not enough on many regional trains, buses, and trams; look for the small validation machine and stamp your ticket before or immediately after boarding.
  • Remember noleggiare (rent a vehicle) vs. affittare (rent a room/ apartment) — they aren't interchangeable, unlike English "rent," which covers both.

Practice

Write a short dialogue at a train station: ask what time and from which platform the train to Venice leaves, then buy and mention validating your ticket.
