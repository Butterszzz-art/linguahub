Dining Out — Reservations, Ordering, and Paying

This lesson walks through a restaurant visit start to finish: reserving a table, understanding the course structure of an Italian meal, and asking for and paying the bill — including a few customs that differ sharply from North America.

Grammar

  • To reserve a table, use vorrei + prenotare (to reserve): Vorrei prenotare un tavolo per due, per favore. (I'd like to reserve a table for two, please.)
  • A full Italian meal traditionally moves through courses in this order: antipasto (appetizer) -> primo (first course — pasta, risotto, or soup) -> secondo (second course — meat or fish) -> contorno (side dish/ vegetable, ordered separately) -> dolce (dessert). You are never obligated to order every course.
  • To ask for the bill, use portare (to bring) or fare (to make/do): Ci porta il conto, per favore? (Will you bring us the bill, please? — formal) or simply Il conto, per favore! (The bill, please!) — the server will not bring it unheeded; you must ask.
  • Restaurant vocabulary you'll need: il cameriere/la cameriera (waiter/ waitress), il tavolo (table), il menù (menu), il conto (the bill).

Examples

Vorrei prenotare un tavolo per quattro, per favore.

vohr-RAY preh-noh-TAH-reh oon TAH-voh-loh pehr KWAHT-troh, pehr fah-VOH-reh

I'd like to reserve a table for four, please.

Come primo prendo le lasagne, come secondo il pollo.

KOH-meh PREE-moh PREHN-doh leh lah-ZAH-nyeh, KOH-meh seh-KOHN-doh eel POHL-loh

For my first course I'll have lasagna, for my second the chicken.

Il conto, per favore!

eel KOHN-toh, pehr fah-VOH-reh

The bill, please!

Si paga anche il coperto.

see PAH-gah AHN-keh eel koh-PEHR-toh

There's also a cover charge.

Notes

  • Tipping is not customary in Italy — the coperto (a small per-person cover charge) covers bread and table service instead, and it appears on nearly every restaurant bill; it isn't a mistake or a scam.
  • Servers consider it rude to bring the bill before you ask for it — they won't rush you, so you need to actively request il conto when you're ready to leave.

Practice

Role-play a restaurant reservation call (name, party size, time), then order a full meal naming one dish for each course.
