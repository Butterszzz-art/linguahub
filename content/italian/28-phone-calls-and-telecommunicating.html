Phone Calls and Telecommunicating

Phone etiquette has its own script in every language. This lesson covers how Italians answer and open a phone call, ask who's calling, and handle messages — plus the vocabulary for phones and phone cards.

Grammar

  • Italians answer the phone with Pronto? (literally "ready?") rather than a greeting — this is universal and worth internalizing as a fixed phrase.
  • To identify yourself on the phone, Italian uses essere (Lesson 9), not a direct "this is": Sono Marco. (This is Marco. — lit. "I am Marco.")
  • To ask who's calling: Chi parla? (Who's speaking?) or, more formally, Con chi parlo? (With whom am I speaking?)
  • To ask for someone: C'è [name]? (Is [name] there?) To say someone just stepped out: È appena uscito/a. (He/she just went out.)
  • Leaving or requesting a message: Posso lasciare un messaggio? (Can I leave a message?) Le posso lasciare un messaggio? (Can I leave him/her a message? — formal, using the indirect object pronoun le)
  • Asking to call back: Ti posso richiamare più tardi? (informal, "Can I call you back later?") La posso richiamare fra mezz'ora? (formal, "Can I call you back in half an hour?")

Examples

Pronto? Sono Giorgio, c'è Simona?

PROHN-toh? SOH-noh JOHR-joh, cheh see-MOH-nah

Hello? This is Giorgio, is Simona there?

Mi dispiace, è in riunione. Posso lasciare un messaggio?

mee dees-PYAH-cheh, eh een ryoo-NYOH-neh. POHS-soh lah-SHAH-reh oon mehs-SAHJ-joh

I'm sorry, he's in a meeting. Can I leave a message?

Ti posso richiamare più tardi?

tee POHS-soh ree-kyah-MAH-reh pyoo TAHR-dee

Can I call you back later?

Con chi parlo, scusi?

kohn kee PAHR-loh, SKOO-zee

With whom am I speaking, excuse me?

Notes

  • Answering with "Pronto?" instead of a name or greeting is a fixed cultural convention — using buongiorno as your very first word on answering the phone will sound unusual to a native speaker.
  • Sono + [your name] is the standard way to identify yourself, mirroring how essere is used for identity throughout the language (Sono americano, Sono studente, Sono Marco).
