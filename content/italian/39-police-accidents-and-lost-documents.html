Police, Accidents, and Lost Documents

This lesson covers a different kind of emergency vocabulary: reporting an accident, dealing with the police, and handling a lost or stolen passport or other documents.

Grammar

  • To report that something has happened, use the passato prossimo (Lessons 26-27): C'è stato un incidente. (There's been an accident.) Note stato is the past participle of essere itself, used here impersonally.
  • Asking and describing injury: Ci sono feriti? (Is anyone injured?) C'è una persona ferita. (There's an injured person.)
  • To report something stolen or lost, mi hanno rubato (they stole from me / [thing] was stolen from me) is a common indirect construction: Mi hanno rubato il passaporto. (My passport was stolen. — lit. "They stole my passport from me.") For something simply lost (not stolen): Ho perso il passaporto. (I lost my passport.)
  • Italy's emergency police number is 113, valid nationwide, and reachable for police assistance as well as ambulance dispatch in an emergency.
  • Useful phrases for dealing with authorities: Ho bisogno di un avvocato. (I need a lawyer.) C'è un interprete che parla inglese? (Is there an interpreter who speaks English?)

Examples

C'è stato un incidente in Piazza Mattei.

cheh STAH-toh oon een-chee-DEHN-teh een PYAHT-tsah maht-TEH-ee

There's been an accident in Piazza Mattei.

Mi hanno rubato il portafoglio sull'autobus.

mee AHN-noh roo-BAH-toh eel pohr-tah-FOH-lyoh sool-LOU-toh-boos

My wallet was stolen on the bus.

Devo denunciare il furto alla polizia.

DEH-voh deh-noon-CHAH-reh eel FOOR-toh AHL-lah poh-lee-TSEE-ah

I need to report the theft to the police.

Ho perso il passaporto, cosa devo fare?

oh PEHR-soh eel pahs-sah- POHR-toh, KOH-zah DEH-voh FAH-reh

I've lost my passport, what should I do?

Notes

  • Keep "ho perso" (I lost) and "mi hanno rubato" (it was stolen from me) distinct — they have real practical consequences (insurance, police reports), so don't use them interchangeably.
  • If you lose or have a passport stolen, contacting your country's embassy or consulate is the standard next step after reporting to local police — worth knowing even though it's outside the language lesson itself.

Practice

Write a short police report dialogue: describe what happened (an accident or theft), whether anyone was hurt, and what was taken or lost.
