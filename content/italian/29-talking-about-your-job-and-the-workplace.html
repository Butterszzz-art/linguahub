Talking About Your Job and the Workplace

Talking about work is common small talk once introductions are out of the way. This lesson covers professions, workplace vocabulary, and the verb lavorare (to work) alongside fare (to do) for describing what someone does for a living.

Grammar

  • To ask what someone does for work: Che lavoro fai/fa? (What work do you do? inf./form.) or Che cosa fai nella vita? (What do you do [for a living]?)
  • To answer, Italian typically uses fare + the profession (with an article) or essere + the profession (without an article) — both are correct, with essere being slightly more formal/simple:
    Faccio l'insegnante. / Sono insegnante. (I'm a teacher.)
    Fa il medico. / È medico. (He/she is a doctor.)
  • Many profession nouns have distinct masculine/feminine forms, following the same patterns as other nouns (Lesson 4): l'avvocato/l'avvocatessa (lawyer), il dottore/la dottoressa (doctor), l'infermiere/l'infermiera (nurse); some are invariable across gender, changing only the article: il/la dentista, il/la giornalista.
  • Lavorare (to work) is a regular -are verb (Lesson 8): lavoro, lavori, lavora, lavoriamo, lavorate, lavorano. It pairs with per (for) or in (at/in) to describe where: Lavoro per un'azienda italiana. (I work for an Italian company.) Lavoro in un ufficio. (I work in an office.)

Examples

Che lavoro fai? Faccio l'insegnante.

keh lah-VOH-roh fahy? FAHCH-choh leen-seh-NYAHN-teh

What do you do for work? I'm a teacher.

Lavoro in un ufficio dal lunedì al venerdì.

lah-VOH-roh een oon oof-FEE-choh dahl loo-neh-DEE ahl veh-nehr-DEE

I work in an office Monday through Friday.

Vorrei prendere un appuntamento.

vohr-RAY PREHN-deh-reh oon ahp-poon-tah-MEHN-toh

I'd like to make an appointment.

Mia sorella è avvocato a Milano.

MEE-ah soh-REHL-lah eh ahv-voh-KAH-toh ah mee-LAH-noh

My sister is a lawyer in Milan.

Notes

  • Whichever pattern you use (fare + article, or essere without one), stay consistent — mixing them mid-sentence ("Sono l'insegnante") is the kind of small error that's easy to avoid once you notice it.
  • Some profession words remain the same for men and women, distinguished only by the article (il dentista / la dentista) — don't force a feminine ending onto every profession noun.

Practice

Describe your own job or a family member's job, including where they work and what days/hours.
