####

HOW THIS COURSE IS BUILT

This curriculum blends two approaches: the topical, conversational structure of a "For Dummies" travel-and-life guide, and the systematic grammar progression of a university grammar workbook. Grammar-heavy lessons build your engine (verbs, cases, word order); topical lessons give you fuel (vocabulary and real conversations for daily situations). They're interleaved on purpose — you'll use each new grammar tool almost immediately in a real-world lesson.

German rewards a slightly different learning order than French or Italian: the case system (nominative, accusative, dative, genitive) is the backbone of the language, so it appears early and gets reinforced constantly. Word order — especially verb-second and verb-final rules — is the other recurring thread.

Files are delivered part by part. This index will be updated as each part is completed.
